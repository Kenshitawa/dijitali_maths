Upcoming Events & Mathematics Festivals

Join our holiday programmes, educator workshops, and community math festivals across Nairobi and Nakuru.

Festival
18 Oct

Nakuru Community Maths Fest

Nakuru County Grounds, Nakuru For: Learners, Families & Educators 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM

An outdoor festival packed with giant tangrams, math obstacle relays, puzzle stations, and hands-on resource creation booths for parents and kids.

Holiday Camp
04 Nov

Dijitali Holiday Maths Discovery Bootcamp

Kayole 1 Comprehensive School, Nairobi For: Primary & Junior Secondary Learners (Ages 8–15) 5-Day Intensive (Mon–Fri)

A 5-day holiday experience exploring mathematics games, digital tablet quests, and binary code craft activities in a supportive environment.

Educator PD
22 Nov

Hands-On Low-Cost Math Aids Workshop

Nairobi Teacher Training Resource Center For: Mathematics Teachers & School Heads 10:00 AM – 2:30 PM

Practical session where teachers construct reusable classroom aids (such as painted counting cups and geometric manipulatives) from low-cost materials.

Virtual
06 Dec

Webinar: Overcoming Math Anxiety in the Classroom

Live Online (Zoom) For: Teachers, Parents & Education Stakeholders 6:00 PM – 7:30 PM EAT

Founder Brenda Atieno shares strategies on shifting mindset from “I'm not a maths person” to curiosity and resilience through learner-centred instruction.
