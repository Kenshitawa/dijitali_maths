What We Offer & How We Teach

From learner holiday bootcamps and mathematical challenges to practical teacher workshops and festive community maths days, discover our comprehensive programmes.

Programme Area A

Learner Sessions & Holiday Programmes

Structured mathematics experiences designed to help children build lasting confidence and intuitive conceptual understanding. Sessions blend physical game-based activities with interactive digital tablet learning.

Programme Area B

Mathematics Challenges & Enrichment

Enrichment activities allowing learners to stretch mathematical thinking far beyond routine classroom drills. We emphasize mathematical communication, pattern discovery, creative problem solving, and collaborative reasoning.

Signature Activity Example: The Binary Code Necklace Activity, where students translate letters into 8-bit binary numbers using colored beads, merging computer science principles with powers of 2.

Logical Reasoning Creative Mathematics Independent Thinking Binary & Patterns
Programme Area D

Teacher Workshops & Practical Support

Dijitali works directly with teachers to explore practical, enjoyable, and low-cost ways of making mathematics accessible. We demonstrate how teachers can construct reusable mathematical tools from locally sourced materials.

  • Creating low-cost teaching aids (e.g., hand-painted counting cups)
  • Games-based mathematical classroom routines
  • Adapting instruction for diverse learning speeds and styles
  • Using digital tools meaningfully without tech overwhelm
Book a Teacher Workshop
Programme Area E

Community Mathematics Activities & Festivals

We bring learners, families, educators, and community partners together around mathematics through joyful outdoor festivals, math stations, family challenges, and school showcase events.

Events such as our Community Maths Days feature giant floor tangram puzzles, relay math races, geometry discovery tents, and parent-child problem-solving challenges.

Emerging Programme • In Development

Dijitali Mathematics Clubs

Dijitali is currently piloting and experimenting with dedicated after-school and community mathematics club formats. In these clubs, learners regularly engage with mathematics through games, peer projects, recurring challenges, and collaborative activities.

Incubator Note: We are currently testing different club structures with partner schools in Nairobi and Nakuru to establish the most impactful model before broader rollout.
