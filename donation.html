How You Can Help

More Ways to Back Our Mission

Beyond financial donations, there are many ways to support Dijitali Maths and the children we serve.

Direct Impact

Sponsor a Learner Experience

Support a child’s participation in structured mathematics sessions, holiday discovery bootcamps, and STEM enrichment challenges.

Hands-On Aids

Fund Learning Materials

Help us create and distribute reusable hands-on manipulatives, math games, and low-cost counting cups to underserved classrooms.

Ecosystem Growth

Support a Maths Club

Fund the setup of recurring after-school mathematics club pilots where children explore problem solving and collaborative mathematics projects.

Research & Pedagogy

Back Math Innovation

Help us pilot and evaluate new approaches to mathematics instruction, digital tools, and learner anxiety reduction strategies.

Institutional Alliance

Strategic Partnership

Organizations can collaborate through multi-year funding, venue access, educational technology, research partnerships, or corporate volunteering.

Amplify

Spread the Word

Share our story with friends, family, schools, and employers. Your network could be a learner’s biggest breakthrough.
