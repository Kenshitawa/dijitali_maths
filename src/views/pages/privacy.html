Privacy Policy & Data Safeguarding

How Dijitali Maths collects, protects, and respects user information in Kenya.

1. Commitment to Privacy & Child Safeguarding

At Dijitali Maths, we are committed to protecting the privacy of our learners, parents, educators, volunteers, and partners. When interacting with young learners, we strictly follow child protection ethics and data minimization standards in accordance with the Data Protection Act of Kenya.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that is voluntarily submitted through our website forms, including contact details (Full Name, Email address, Phone/WhatsApp number), volunteer application details (skills, availability, motivation), event registrations, and newsletter email addresses.

3. Privacy-First Visitor Analytics

We measure aggregate website visits without tracking cookies or third-party ad trackers. We never store raw IP addresses: unique daily visitor counts are estimated using a daily salted cryptographic hash that cannot be reversed, and raw IP addresses are immediately discarded.

4. Embedded Videos & Donations

The homepage loads a muted autoplay video from YouTube when opened, which connects your browser to YouTube and may share technical request data with that provider. If you choose M-Pesa Express, your phone number and donation amount are sent to Safaricom for the payment request; we retain the amount, payment result/receipt, and only the phone number's last four digits.

5. Photography & Media Consent

Dijitali Maths captures photographs and video during learning sessions and public festivals to celebrate learner achievements and train educators. Written parental or guardian consent is always obtained for learner media, and all uploaded photos have EXIF/GPS metadata stripped.
