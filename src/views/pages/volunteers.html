Ways To Engage

How You Can Be Part Of Dijitali

For Parents & Learners

Enrol your child in our holiday discovery camps, participate in weekend community maths days, and explore creative math challenges at home.

For Teachers

Attend practical workshops on games-based instruction, discover low-cost manipulatives, and join a peer network of innovative educators.

For Schools

Bring Dijitali Maths into your school through math enrichment programmes, math club pilots, and customized teacher professional development.

For Volunteers

Share your time, energy, and creative skills in tutoring, event coordination, content design, research, or photography. (No math degree required!)

For Partners

Collaborate on STEM initiatives, joint research, co-hosted festivals, and community learning outreach programs in Kenya.

For Donors & Supporters

Support learning materials, sponsor learner experiences, fund math club development, and back innovative approaches to mathematics learning.
