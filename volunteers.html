Volunteer With Us

Do you love mathematics, learning and working with young people?

Dijitali Maths welcomes people who want to contribute their skills, time, and creativity towards making mathematics more engaging for learners. You don't have to be a mathematics teacher to volunteer!

Depending on current programmes, volunteers may support learner sessions, maths challenges, community events, mathematics clubs, holiday bootcamps, teacher workshops, content development, research, or creative media projects.
