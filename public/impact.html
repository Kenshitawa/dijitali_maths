Core Transformation

The Four Mindset Shifts

Stage 1

From Fear

“I can't do maths” — We break down intimidation with games.

Stage 2

To Curiosity

“Let me try” — Tactile manipulatives spark experimentation without fear.

Stage 3

To Mastery

“I understand” — Through guided reflection, concepts click naturally.

Stage 4

To Leadership

“Let me show someone else” — Learners explain reasoning to peers and siblings.
