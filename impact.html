Core Transformation

The Four Mindset Shifts

How our learner-centred methodology systematically dismantles math anxiety.

Stage 1: From Fear

“I can't do maths”

Learners often arrive convinced they are not "math people" after struggling with rote procedural drills. We break down the intimidation with games.

Stage 2: To Curiosity

“Let me try”

Tactile manipulatives, colorful blocks, and low-stakes challenges spark willingness to experiment and test hypotheses without fear of failure.

Stage 3: To Mastery

“I understand”

Through guided reflection and discovery, concepts click naturally. Learners recognize mathematical logic intuitively.

Stage 4: To Leadership

“Let me show someone else” 🌟

The ultimate test of confidence: learners enthusiastically explain mathematical reasoning to peers and siblings at home.

Geographic Reach

Nairobi & Nakuru County Footprint

Expanding from community schools in Nairobi to festivals and educator workshops across the Rift Valley.

Origin Hub

Nairobi County

Home to our founding classrooms in Kayole and surrounding comprehensive schools. Focused on holiday maths bootcamps, tablet-assisted learning, and grassroots learner sessions.

  • 600+ Learners engaged in school sessions
  • Weekly holiday programmes & enrichment
  • Binary necklace & computing integration
Regional Expansion

Nakuru County

A thriving hub for community maths festivals, teacher development workshops, and low-cost manipulative resource creation for rural and suburban schools.

  • 400+ Teachers equipped with practical aids
  • Nakuru Community Maths Festival coordination
  • Reusable counting cups distribution
